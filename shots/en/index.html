memex. Screenshots Privacy Policy

Effective: October 21, 2026

memex. Screenshots ("the app") automatically organizes the screenshots on your phone so you can find them again. This policy explains what the app processes, where it is sent, and when it is deleted. The app is separate from the Memex notes app, with separate accounts and data.

Your original photos stay on your device.
The app never moves or deletes originals in your Photos library and never stores original image files on its servers. If you agree to image analysis, a resized copy of the image (longest side up to 1,280 px) and the text read on your device are sent through the app's server to Anthropic's Claude for classification.

1. Information we process

  • Account — account identifier and email address from Sign in with Apple or Google (Apple lets you hide your email).
  • Information derived from screenshots — text read on your device (including the raw text), category and subtopic, summary, keywords, key points, your notes, capture date.
  • Photo reference — an identifier used to find the original in your Photos library again. It is not the photo itself.
  • Classification memory — corrections you chose to "use as a reference next time". Stored in your account so it is restored on other devices.
  • Interest reports — the report content, only if you request one.
  • Feedback — the text you type, feedback type, app version and account identifier. Photos, screenshot text and search queries are never attached automatically.

Kept only on your device: original photos, in-app photo copies, Photos import progress, AI search consent and its query cache.

2. Information we do not process

  • Server storage of original image files
  • Location, contacts, call logs, phone numbers, postal addresses
  • Advertising identifiers or tracking for advertising

3. Purposes

  • Classifying screenshots into categories and subtopics, creating summaries and keywords so you can find them again
  • Using your corrections as references for future classification
  • Natural-language search (if you agree) and interest reports (if you request them)
  • Storing your data in your account, fixing problems and handling feedback

Data is sent to Anthropic over the internet each time you use one of these features and is processed and retained under Anthropic's commercial terms and privacy policy. The app does not separately store images it sends to Anthropic. Image analysis, natural-language search and interest reports each work only after you agree; otherwise that information is not sent.

5. Consent and withdrawal

  • Image analysis — you agree when starting Photos import. You can turn automatic import off on the Photos import screen.
  • AI search — off by default; turn it on or off in Settings. When off, queries are not sent and on-device search is used.
  • Classification memory — chosen when you correct a category; clear it anytime in Settings.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Your account and saved information are kept until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Individual items can be deleted in the app and are removed from the server as well.
  • Account deletion: go to Settings → Delete account. Your account and its server data (screenshot information, classification memory, reports, feedback) are deleted and app data on the device is cleaned up. If you use Sign in with Apple, the app confirms it is you and revokes the Apple sign-in link. Originals in your Photos library are not deleted.
  • If you cannot use the app, request deletion by email below.

7. Your rights

You can access, correct and delete your information and ask us to stop processing it, in the app or via the contact below.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Server data is protected by per-account access control (row-level security) so only you can read and write it. AI service keys and other secrets are kept on the server, never in the app.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their information.
